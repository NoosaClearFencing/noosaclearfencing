Website privacy

Updated 25 October 2026

Contacting us

Our call, text and email links open your phone, messaging or email app. Any details you choose to send are used to respond to your enquiry and discuss your fencing project. Please only send information needed for your enquiry.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand website visits and whether visitors select a call, text or email link. The Analytics tag loads only after you choose “Allow analytics”. Choosing “No thanks” keeps it off and does not affect contacting us.

Analytics can process cookie identifiers, device and browser information, page visits and approximate location inferred from your internet connection. Google processes this information on its infrastructure, which may be outside Australia. We do not send your name, phone number, email address, enquiry text, URL query strings or precise location to Analytics. Contact-button clicks do not tell us whether a call connected or a message was sent.

Advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in our website tag. We do not use this tag for remarketing. Learn more about Google’s privacy practices.

Changing your choice

We remember your analytics choice in this browser for up to 180 days. You can change it with the “Analytics choices” button below. Withdrawing permission stops future collection through our tag and clears its Analytics cookies from this site; it does not erase information already received by Google. Clearing your browser storage also resets your choice.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical request information to deliver and protect the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Questions

Email admin@noosaclearfencing.com.au with privacy questions or requests concerning information you have provided to us.
