About Us

Noosa Clear Fencing is a proud Queensland company based in Noosa Heads that proudly installs premium outdoor solutions for the Australian market.

We are leaders in the design and installation of innovative products for glass pool fencing. We offer a wide range of products including frameless and semi-frameless systems.

Our product range is fully compliant with Queensland regulations and Australian Standards AS1926.1. We are QBCC licensed (#15554051) and issue a Form 15 Compliance Certificate with every installation, providing full documentation for councils and specifiers to make compliance easy.

Get in touch with us today to find out how we can make your pool vision a reality.

Testing of glass balustrades to AS/NZS 1170.0/1170.1 and 1170.2 available throughout Australia.
