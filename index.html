GOOD TO KNOW

A few clear
answers.

How do I arrange a quote?

Call 0432 665 927 or email admin@noosaclearfencing.com.au. Include your suburb, a few photos of the pool area and any approximate measurements. We offer a free measure and quote.

Do you supply and install the fencing?

Yes. Noosa Clear Fencing supplies and installs frameless glass pool fencing. We’ll confirm the materials, layout and installation scope in your quote.

What affects the cost?

Fence length, gate layout, chosen hardware, the mounting surface and site access all affect the quote. A site measure helps us provide a price for your particular project.

How soon can my fence be installed?

Timing depends on the project, materials and current bookings. Let us know your preferred dates when you enquire so we can discuss availability.

When can I contact you?

We’re open Monday to Saturday, 7:30 am–5 pm, Queensland time. Closed Sunday. You can email outside those hours and we’ll respond when we’re back.
