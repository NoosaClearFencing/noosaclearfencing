About Us

Noosa Clear Fencing is a proud local Queensland company based in Noosa Heads, specialising in the supply and professional installation of premium frameless and semi-frameless glass pool fencing systems.

We are leaders in delivering elegant, compliant pool fencing solutions that enhance the natural beauty of Sunshine Coast and Noosa properties while ensuring the highest levels of safety for families.

Our Queensland product range is fully compliant with Australian Standards AS1926.1 and Queensland pool safety regulations. We are QBCC licensed (Licence #15554051) and issue a Form 15 Compliance Certificate with every installation.

As a family-owned local business, we provide personalised service, free on-site consultations, accurate quotes, and fast professional installations – typically completed within 2–3 weeks.

Get in touch with us today to find out how we can make your pool area safe and stunning.
